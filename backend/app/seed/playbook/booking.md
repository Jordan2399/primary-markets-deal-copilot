# Booking

Booking is the last stage. The allocated bonds are recorded in the bank's systems, checked against the final terms, and settled with the issuer and the investors.

## Booking the trade

After allocation, the trade is booked in the bank's systems with each investor's allocation. The booked details, which include the size, the coupon, the issue price, the dates and the ISIN, are checked against the signed final terms. Any difference is investigated before settlement. Booking the trade depends on the final terms being signed off.

## ISIN and static data

The ISIN is the international identifier of the bond. It is confirmed together with the other static data that downstream systems need. An ISIN has a check digit, so a mistyped ISIN can be detected automatically.

## Settlement and closing

Settlement happens a few days after pricing, on the date set in the terms. Investors pay against delivery of the bonds through the clearing systems. At closing the issuer receives the net proceeds, which are the proceeds minus the agreed fees. Settlement instructions are sent beforehand and a closing is arranged with the issuer and the agents.

## Fees

The management and underwriting fees and the selling concessions are confirmed and recorded. Fees do not block settlement in this checklist, but they must be confirmed before the final report.

## After the deal

A post-deal report is prepared for DCM and the issuer. It summarises the final book, the allocation by investor type and region, and how the bond performed in the secondary market.

## Exit criteria

The ISIN and static data are confirmed, the trade is booked and checked, and settlement and closing are arranged.
