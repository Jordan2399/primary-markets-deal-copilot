# Launch

Launch is the moment the deal is announced to the market and the order book opens. It is a short, tightly controlled stage because everything that follows depends on it.

## The announcement

The announcement goes out through the usual market channels. It states the issuer, the ratings, the currency, the expected size, the tenor, the format and the initial price thoughts, and tells investors when orders are being taken. The issuer and compliance approve the wording before it is sent.

## The order book opens

Investors place orders, usually with a limit, which is the tightest spread at which they still want to buy. The syndicate desk records every order in the order book and checks that order capture is working. A benchmark-size deal often receives many orders within the first hour.

## Checks before launch

Before the announcement, the deal team confirms that the documents are substantially final, that the issuer has agreed to launch, and that the internal and compliance approvals are in place. The tasks in the Launch stage depend on each other: the announcement comes first, then the initial price thoughts are released, then the book is opened.

## Exit criteria

The announcement is approved and sent, the initial price thoughts have been released and the order book is open and capturing orders correctly. When these tasks are complete the deal moves to Book building.
