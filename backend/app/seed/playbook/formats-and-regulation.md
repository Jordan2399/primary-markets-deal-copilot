# Formats and regulation

This page gives a short orientation on the formats a new issue can take and the rules that shape them. It is a summary for working context and is not legal advice.

## Reg S and 144A

Bonds sold to investors in the United States or to US persons are governed by US securities law. Rule 144A allows resale to qualified institutional buyers in the United States without full registration. Regulation S covers sales outside the United States. A bond can be offered under both, often called 144A/Reg S, so that it reaches US and non-US investors, or under Reg S only for non-US investors. The documents differ: 144A offerings normally involve an offering memorandum and additional legal opinions and letters from counsel.

## Domestic and European offerings

Bonds sold in Europe follow the Prospectus Regulation and the national rules of the countries where they are offered. A minimum denomination of 100,000 euro, common for wholesale bonds, allows lighter disclosure than retail offerings. MiFID II product governance rules also require the manufacturer and the distributors to define a target market for the bond.

## Green and sustainable bonds

In a green bond the issuer commits to using the proceeds for eligible green projects described in a framework. Frameworks are often aligned with the ICMA Green Bond Principles and reviewed by an independent second-party opinion provider. After the deal the issuer reports on how the proceeds were used. For allocation, investors with a sustainability mandate may be given priority.

## Why it matters for the workflow

The chosen format decides which documents are needed in the Documentation stage, which investors can be approached, and sometimes the timing. It is therefore agreed at the end of the Mandate stage.
